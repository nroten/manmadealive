▲ The gap you've been living in

You've been carrying responsibility — without anyone teaching you how to actually grow into it.

Most churches form men through Sunday attendance alone. The Lewis Center calls this the collapse of "faith formation by osmosis" — the assumption that sitting in pews is enough to grow. In a post-pandemic world, most committed men are in church one or two Sundays a month. You've probably already felt that isn't sufficient.

You aren't lazy. You aren't faithless. You're a man carrying real responsibility without real formation — and that is a very different problem with a very different solution.

◆ Why this is rising, not fading

The hunger you feel is everywhere.

Men's church attendance is rising — Barna's 2025 data shows men now outpace women for the first time in recorded survey history. Bible use among men climbed 19% in a single year. Commitment to Christ among Millennial men has risen 19 points since 2019.

"You haven't abandoned your faith. You've been faithful with insufficient tools."

— Man Made Alive

◆ The biblical framework

For lasting change to take root, truth has to travel the full distance.

From your mind, through your heart, and out through your hands. Most things you've tried reached your head. A few reached your heart. Almost none stayed long enough to reach your hands.

That's not because you didn't try hard enough. It's because nothing was built to take you the full way. MMA is built for the full journey — slowly, in order, with brothers walking the same road.

The transformation is the Spirit's work. The pathway is the structured environment in which you cooperate with that work.

"Most programs reach the head. Some reach the heart. Almost none stay long enough to reach the hands."

A man you already are.

◆ The man at the table tonight

Not in distress. Not in joy. Just present — carrying something quietly.

◆ Who you are right now

You're between 28 and 55. Probably married, likely a father. You attend church regularly — maybe even serve. You work. You provide. You carry real weight. You are not lazy, not faithless, not checked out. By almost every external measure, you are a good man.

And yet something feels persistently, quietly off. There is a tension between who you sense you're supposed to be and how you're actually living. You've carried it without naming it. You've suspected you're the only one who feels it. You're not.

"You're a man ready to engage a process that's honest about what formation actually requires — and serious enough to ask something real of you."

— Man Made Alive
▲ What you've been carrying
In your marriage
You love your wife and are often uncertain how to lead her. You avoid conflict because conflict exhausts you. Important things go unsaid for months and surface wrong.
As a father
You know the window with your kids isn't unlimited. You know your presence matters. You don't know how to close the gap between your intentions and your actual impact.
In your friendships
You probably have men you watch football with. You almost certainly don't have a man who knows enough of your story to notice when you start drifting — and say something about it.
In your faith
You're showing up. You're sincere. And you suspect that what you've been calling faith has mostly been attendance — and you don't know how to make it more than that.
